# EU Trust & Green Commerce Suite — App Store listing

## App name
EU Trust & Green Commerce Suite

## Subtitle
GDPR compliance, cookie consent, age verification, VAT/IOSS and carbon-neutral shipping in one app.

## Description
EU Trust & Green Commerce Suite is the all-in-one compliance and sustainability toolkit for Shopify merchants selling in Europe.

Key features:
- GDPR cookie consent banner with analytics, marketing and necessary toggles
- Customer data export and deletion workflow
- Auto-generated privacy policy, terms, impressum, widerruf and AGB
- Cookie scanner to detect third-party scripts
- 18+ age verification popup
- VAT / IOSS display on product and cart pages
- Carbon-neutral shipping calculator
- Carbon offset upsell widget
- Green courier options: DPD, GLS, DHL GoGreen
- Product carbon footprint badge
- Sustainability report dashboard
- Consent audit logs for DSGVO/GDPR
